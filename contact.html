Security disclosures

Responsible disclosure for vulnerabilities in the SDK, gateway, or protocol. Email signal@aeoess.com. Full policy in each repo's SECURITY.md.

IETF, W3C, AAIF, AGT, DIF, ERC-8004. Open an issue on GitHub in the relevant repo, or email for off-thread coordination.

Press and research

Working group coverage, paper questions, academic citation. Email signal@aeoess.com. Public papers indexed at aeoess.com/research.

What we don't do. No phone, no Slack, no Discord. Email is async and gets answered. For technical questions about the SDK, an issue on GitHub usually surfaces faster than email.
