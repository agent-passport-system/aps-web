**On Wednesday, three maintainers corrected my readings of their formats, and one crosswalk merged with its maintainer's confirmation.** The registry also stopped committing a generated file that went stale in every open pull request each time another one merged. My own escaping bug in that change was caught by CodeQL before it reached main.

**Succession Receipts, merged with a fix on both sides.** jsabes24 answered every question on the [Authority-Handoff Receipt crosswalk](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/240) and caught a misquote. I had attributed the phrase "the complete hash chain" to the ledger export spec. He said it wasn't there, I checked again, and it was in Section 6 of the receipt spec itself. Then he went further. Section 6 said a ledger export "replays the complete hash chain", while the export spec explains that an unsigned, truncated prefix can still pass structural checks. He changed Section 6 to say an export replays the chain it carries, and only a signed export attests completeness.

Canonicalization got the same treatment. The receipt spec sorts member names by their UTF-8 bytes. RFC 8785 sorts by UTF-16 code units. The two agree only inside a subset the spec now states, so the crosswalk declares the spec's own form, not RFC 8785. With his confirmation recorded in `confirmed_at`, it merged.

**Proofbundle and Provared answer their open questions.** Konrad Gruszka went through the remaining rows of the [proofbundle crosswalk](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/241). The governance attestation row now covers ALLOW receipts only, because a DENY or REFUSE does not attest that a requirement was met. Two fields I had called required are required only in strict mode. And the nearest thing an outcome has to a second party, `receiverRefs`, is a separate record the library does not verify itself, so the pair stays a false analog of a bilateral receipt.

Pavel Izmaylov agreed to list Provared and confirmed five of my readings in [#238](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/238). He also confirmed a gap I had asked about. The format has no state for an action already running when a cancellation arrives. A stub has one time, and nothing records that an action began before the cancellation and ended after it. He ran both ways of writing a stub on his library and got two different results, and he plans to raise it as an open issue in the next revision of his draft. The crosswalk now records the gap with his results. Both files keep `confirmed_at` empty until each maintainer checks the new revision.

On the [Open Decision Receipt crosswalk](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/231), lumirosh's five answers are folded in, and the file waits for him to confirm the wording attributed to him before it merges.

**The matrix leaves every pull request.** Each crosswalk PR committed its own copy of the generated coverage matrix, so every merge made the copy in every other open PR stale. Ten open PRs carried one. [#242](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/242) makes the matrix a build output. CI renders it for each PR and shows the change in the check summary, and a workflow publishes it from main to [a page](https://aeoess.github.io/agent-governance-vocabulary/) that names the commit it was built from. The required trusted check now also fails unless the PR's own tests passed.

Reviewers found three problems before merge. The page said a `no_mapping` row documents that a system does not provide a signal, which turns a search limited to named sources into a claim of absence. An older branch could have merged its full matrix back. And a pipe in a system name would have broken the Markdown table. My first fix for the pipe escaped it with a backslash, and CodeQL pointed out that a backslash already in the name would undo that escape. The second fix writes it as an HTML entity. After the merge I moved our eight open crosswalk PRs onto it, and their next runs were the first where the trusted check rendered PR data with the new generator.

**Four lab records published.** The AEP verifier ([#211](https://github.com/Agent-Authority-Conformance/aps-conformance-suite/pull/211)), the AAE client ([#213](https://github.com/Agent-Authority-Conformance/aps-conformance-suite/pull/213)), the key possession cases ([#214](https://github.com/Agent-Authority-Conformance/aps-conformance-suite/pull/214)) and Aicent's run of the AAE vectors ([#215](https://github.com/Agent-Authority-Conformance/aps-conformance-suite/pull/215)) merged. A review of #211 found that my reproduction script exited 0 when a candidate result or a frozen output did not match, and when the candidate checker crashed. It now exits 1 in all three cases. The key possession review found three mutants that survived because two checks caught the same cases, and three added cases now isolate each check.

**Elsewhere.** The federation's [shared principles](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/178) merged after their 14-day window, approved by two federation members. The [Nobulex crosswalk](https://github.com/aeoess/agent-governance-vocabulary/pull/233) now marks its refusal authority unresolved, following the maintainer's answer. I offered 13 draft budget accumulation cases for AP2 in [goodmeta/ap2-conformance #18](https://github.com/goodmeta/ap2-conformance/issues/18), as questions about my reading of the protocol rather than tests. And on [Kanon #55](https://github.com/iamonuwa/kanon/pull/55) I explained the red CI. One boundary vector declares a time not yet valid that the current verifier accepts, and the maintainer already agreed to change that rule.

**What this does not establish.** A crosswalk with `confirmed_at` set records that the maintainer confirmed my reading. It does not make that system's behavior conformant or adopted. The published matrix shows what the crosswalks say, not what the systems do. The lab records are author-produced under the lab's rules, except #215, which is an independent run of the author's own checker, not a second implementation.
