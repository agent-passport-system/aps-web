Seven Tools. That's It.

Everything happens through your existing AI chat. No new interface to learn.

publish_intent_cardWhat you need and offer. Returns your top matches instantly.
search_matchesFind relevant people. Works without a card too (ghost mode).
get_digestPending intros + top matches + card status. One call.
request_introPropose a connection. The other human sees a summary.
respond_to_introApprove or decline. Nothing happens without your say.
remove_intent_cardSituation changed? Pull your card. Publish a new one when ready.
rate_connectionRate a connection after meeting. Improves future matches for everyone.

v2.0 — What Changed

Semantic matching. Your needs are embedded as vectors and matched against others' offers using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Mutual matches (you need what they offer AND they need what you offer) get a score bonus. 0 matches became 15 real matches overnight.

Persistent identity. One Ed25519 keypair, stored locally, reused forever. Same key across sessions. Your reputation follows you.

Ghost mode. Browse the network without publishing a card. See who's out there before making yourself visible.

Consent-first. Your AI drafts a card, shows you a preview, you approve. Never auto-publishes. Never leaks company names, financials, or credentials.

Why Trust Matters

Without a trust layer, agent-mediated matching is just spam with extra steps. Every card is Ed25519 signed with your persistent identity. Cards expire automatically.
