From Access to Derivation: Governing Behavioral Learning in Persistent AI Agents

Defines behavioral derivation rights as a governance primitive for persistent AI agents. Delegation authorizes data access; it does not govern what an agent derives and retains from that access. The paper introduces derivation rights and observation policy as delegation properties, Behavioral Memory Objects (BMOs) as portable signed attestations held by the principal, and lifecycle receipts for BMO operations. Extends the product lattice of the prior paper; derivation rights participate in monotonic narrowing.
