Boilerplate

AI agents act for people and companies, but the systems around them cannot prove the basics: who authorized it, what it was allowed to do, and what happened. AEOESS builds Agent Passport System, an Apache 2.0 open protocol that gives agents a verifiable identity, ties it to the person or company it represents, and enforces scope, budget and conditions of its authority. Every permitted action gets a signed receipt, every denied action leaves evidence, producing a cryptographic trail of authority, enforcement and action. The commercial gateway turns receipts into audit-ready evidence the EU AI Act and enterprise buyers increasingly require.
